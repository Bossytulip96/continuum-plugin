# Mirror the day

For a brain that cannot see its own progress. The mirror puts the record beside the feeling and stops. It does not flatter (praise with no receipt gets discounted) and it does not argue with the feeling. Read only.

1. **Fix the window.** Default is today. "This week" means Monday to now. Another range if the person names one.
2. **Read the receipts:** every line in the window from `Log/decisions-*.md` (all machines), the `about` line and last messages of each `Log/sessions/` digest updated in the window, the dated lines in `Home.md`, and any note whose `last_used` falls in the window. Never read the session transcript itself.
3. **Sort into four piles:** shipped (a thing now exists or is decided), invisible (work that settled something but left no artifact), open (started, not finished), ruled out (looked at and dropped, on the person's word). Ruled-out items count; a decision not to do something is a decision.
4. **Say it back:** one honest bottom line, then the shipped list, then the invisible list, each line at most 15 words with its receipt in parentheses: (decision log 8:17 PM), (Home.md), (note name). Five lines per pile at most; "and N more" for the rest. Open and ruled out as one line each with counts.
5. **If the piles are thin:** say so plainly and name the cause from the record (a wait on someone else, a day off, a plan with no first step). Never "you should have". Never a suggested next task; this skill reports, it does not assign.
