# Pin an idea

1. Restate the thought in one clear sentence and confirm its meaning when unclear.
2. Check existing notes and idea lists for the same thought.
3. Append a dated item to the matching list under notes-and-ideas, with context and any open question.
4. Report the exact note and item. Do not execute the idea without approval.
